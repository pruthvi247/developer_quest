Checklist in conversation:

`Asking questions`: Understand your conflict partner's interests and goals

`Paraphrasing`: Double check your understanding

`Mirroring` : Signals to your conflict partner what they have said is potentially disruptive and subtly invite them to explain them selves

`Labeling` : Build Tactical empathy by reframing the feeling or the need of the other person
