### Three elements
1. hero
2. Villan

### Create a great story
1. Develop a relatable character
2. Include a relevant challenge
3. Demonstrate an honest struggle


- Avoid super man effect
- **Demonstrate honest struggle** - because with out struggle there is nothing interesting about hero or story
### Choose the right story
Step 1: Define your objective
Step 2: Look for a relevant success, failure or moment of clarity
Step 3: If you can't think of a story, make one up
Step 4: Pick the best story

# Story telling
[linkedin-source](https://www.linkedin.com/learning/the-3-minute-rule-say-less-to-get-more/the-3-minute-rule?autoSkip=true&resume=false&u=57692769)
**3-min-rule**
- They must conceptualize your idea
- Contextualize how it will benefit them
- Actualize it with potential engagement for further interest

Separate things from what you want to say to what you need to say

question your self
- what you do 
- what you do well
- what is it
- why is it good

### WHAC method
W - what is it
H- How does it work
A- are you sure (should show some data)
C- can you do it

Your audience must first understand the offering,
`what you do , what it is, why it exists`
must be simple and stand out

Once audience get the Context, then they need
- Understand what it can do for them
- How is this going to help me
- how to i take action
- How i make this happen
- How is this gonna be executed
- who delivered
- what does it cost

Conceptualize : what is it and how does it work ? - Explain the offering
Contextualize : are you sure, is it true - Engage and verify details
Actualize: can you do it  - encourage the buy-in

Some important questions to have answers in hand
**W**
- what make you unique
- What can you do that others can't
- What is the biggest need it fills
- Are there big monetary advantages to your methods
- What problem does this solve
- Who is helped the most by this
- Why does this have to happen now
- What will be different after buying in 
- What hole in the market does this fill
- What could this be worth in success
- why is it low risk
- What makes your competition inferior
- Why can't some one copy you
- what allows you to make your offering work
- Why haven't others used this method
-  Who is actually performing the service
- What are the things that only you know how to do 
- Why are there no other ways to do this
- Why would you choose this method over others
- Why is your way the only way
- who are you using to deliver this
- What do your reviews say
**H**
- How does it work
- How easy is this to implement
- How can you deliver on your promise
- How long will what you propose take
- How many people have this problem
- Has this been done successfully in the past
- How is this safe
- How much money will some one save
- How can this result be replicated
- How do you know there's a need for this
- How valuable is this market
- How have people succeeded in this before
**A**
- Are you taking any shortcuts
- What have you said that some one might not believe
- Has the third party verified your claims
- What in your history confirms this
- What makes you so sure you are right
- How did you know you were onto something
- why is this not too good to be true
- Has anyone lost money like this
- Has any of this been announced in public
- Do you have any unexpected supporters
- Why can't your competition do this better
**C**
- Can you do it

- What have you done that's similar
- Why don't the regulations apply
- Why isn't this restricted
- Is there anything in your past that would ruin this
- How have others failed trying something like this
- How have you trained for this
- Are there other steps to take before you can deliver
- Are there any other third parties involved
- What successes led you to making this work
- Do you have this in posssesion now
- What do you do if someone changes his or her mind
- Do you have the connections that are necessary 
- Is there anyone better suited to deliver
- What are the repercussions of the underperformance
- Who does some one contact if there's a problem
- How have you dealt with problems in the past
