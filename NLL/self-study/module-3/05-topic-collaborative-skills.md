#### Interactions
- _Decision making,_ including complex or uncertain decisions (for example, investment decisions) and cross-cutting routine decisions (such as quarterly business reviews)
- _Creative solutions and coordination,_ including innovation sessions (for example, developing new products) and routine working sessions (such as daily check-ins)
- _Information sharing,_ including one-way communication (video, for instance) and two-way communication (such as town halls with Q&As)
