**Accounting system**:
A Planned process for providing financial info that will be useful to management so they can take informed decision
**Accounting Records**:
Organised summaries of a business's financial activities, Inaccurate records often contribute to business failures
**Financial statement** :
Financial reports that summarise the financial condition and operations of a business

Questions for Analyzing Transactions
1. Which accounts are affected ? - See the chart of accounts for a list
2. How is each account classified ? - Asset, liability, or owners equity
3. How is each Changed ? - increase or decrease
4. How is each entered ? - Debit or credit

**Journal** :
A permanent record of the debit and credit parts of each transaction recorded in chronological order
**Ledger**:
A group of accounts

**General Ledger**
A ledger that contains all accounts needed to prepare financial statements
