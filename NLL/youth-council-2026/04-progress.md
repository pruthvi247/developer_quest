
# Intro pitch

==The hardest part of the problem-solving isn't finding the answer, It's making sure you are solving the right problem.==
It is not just about increasing store participation. It is about redesigning how RetailCo turns frontline observations into repeatable business value.

> Turn frontline observations into tested solutions, then turn proven solutions into organizational habits.


# Elevator Pitch

## The Hospital

A hospital does not perform surgery on every patient who arrives.

It first:
1. Observes symptoms
2. Diagnoses the condition
3. Selects the treatment
4. Monitors recovery

RetailCo should treat ideas similarly.

> **Do not prescribe before diagnosing. Do not scale before testing.**


The solution is a four-stage **Customer Truth Flywheel**:

> **Observe the problem:** _What customer truth are we missing?_
> **Diagnose its value:** _Is this an important problem or merely an interesting idea?_ ==Separate signal from noise==
> **Shape the solution:** _What is the smallest experiment that could prove or disprove the solution?_
> **Scale what works:** _How do we turn a successful experiment into a repeatable habit?_
