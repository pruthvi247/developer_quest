### **Annual Strategy Refresh**

While the core tenets of our strategy remain stable over time, such as our company’s purpose and vision, our strategic priorities get refreshed annually to incorporate the most recent insights and priorities.

#### Ensuring consistency while incorporating latest insights
![[Pasted image 20260223145337.png]]
#### Input Factors

Several key input factors drive our strategy refresh:

- **Feedback from Key Stakeholders**: This includes input from customers, employees, partners, investors, and analysts, ensuring our strategy is comprehensive and inclusive.
- **Market Trends and Customer Needs**: Understanding the evolving market dynamics and customer requirements is crucial for staying competitive.
- **Technology Trends**: We continuously monitor and analyze emerging technology trends to ensure our strategy leverages the latest advancements.
- **Key Achievements and Areas for Improvement**: We assess our past performance to identify what worked well and where we need to improve.

#### Strategy Cascade

Once the strategy is refreshed, it is cascaded throughout the organization at several levels of detail, these include:

- **Corporate Strategy:** The overarching strategy that guides the entire company.
- **Functional and Board Area Strategies:** Tailored strategies for different functional areas and board responsibilities.
- **E2E Solution Area Strategies:** End-to-end strategies for specific solution areas.
- **Semester Plans:** Detailed plans for each semester to ensure focused execution.

#### Company-wide Roll-out

The refreshed strategy is rolled out company-wide through multiple assets and formats, including:

- **Strategy Whitepaper:** A comprehensive document detailing the strategy.
- **Strategy Pitch:** A concise presentation of the strategy.
- **Strategy Website:** An online resource for all strategy-related information.
- **Global Employee Meetings:** Sessions to communicate the strategy to all employees.
- **Functional Kick-off Meetings:** Meetings to align functional areas with the strategy.
- **Learning & Enablement Offerings:** Training sessions to ensure everyone understands and can execute the strategy.

By following this structured approach, we ensure that our strategy is not only well-informed and comprehensive but also effectively communicated and executed across the organization. This process helps us stay agile and responsive to the ever-changing business environment.

## [**Discover AI**](https://sap.sharepoint.com/sites/210068/SitePages/E2E-AI-Lifecycle-Discover-AI.aspx)

[](https://sap.sharepoint.com/sites/210068/SitePages/E2E-Enterprise-AI-Lifecycle.aspx?csf=1&web=1&e=ghePDT&CID=cee21d85-538b-421b-9813-5aa1ad6e361b#discover-ai "Permalink for Discover AI")

The **Discover AI** is the initial phase in the AI use case lifecycle, where ideas for AI-driven solutions are proposed to address organizational needs. This phase focuses on identifying **key business challenges**, **uncovering opportunities**, and **evaluating the potential value** that AI can deliver to the organization.

In this phase, you can **submit your internal AI use case ideas**, detailing the business problem or opportunity you aim to address. Before submission, we highly encourage you to **ideate** about new relevant, reliable, and responsible AI solutions inside your business area (fitting SAPs Business AI Strategy).

Additionally, as part of the CPIT AI use case submission process every new AI use case requires a **high-level business value assessment** to estimate the value potential.

As a result, this phase sets the stage for more in-depth evaluation and execution, ensuring that the most promising and impactful ideas are prioritized first.

## [**Design AI**](https://sap.sharepoint.com/sites/210068/SitePages/E2E-AI-Lifecycle-Design-AI.aspx)

[](https://sap.sharepoint.com/sites/210068/SitePages/E2E-Enterprise-AI-Lifecycle.aspx?csf=1&web=1&e=ghePDT&CID=cee21d85-538b-421b-9813-5aa1ad6e361b#design-ai "Permalink for Design AI")

The primary goal of the **Design AI** phase is to move from an idea to an actionable plan, ensuring that all critical elements - such as business objectives, technical requirements, solution architecture, and use case scope - are clearly defined and aligned. By creating and confirming the solution architecture in this phase, the team ensures that the proposed approach is both viable and capable of achieving the desired outcomes.

Additionally, as part of the CPIT AI use case refinement process for each Big Bet* **a detailed business case is required** in Wave tool. The financial impact needs to be broken down on a monthly basis in the tool for Quality Gate 3 approval. The final case is signed off by business leaders, controlling and value management.​

As a result, this phase ensures that the use case is not only strategically aligned but also technically viable, setting the foundation for successful development in the next phase.

*Big Bets are use cases with high value potential and strong strategic relevance for the business operations (approved by the business operational leads), therefore are being prioritized in the development backlog.

## [**Deliver AI**](https://sap.sharepoint.com/sites/210068/SitePages/E2E-AI-Lifecycle-Deliver-AI.aspx)

[](https://sap.sharepoint.com/sites/210068/SitePages/E2E-Enterprise-AI-Lifecycle.aspx?csf=1&web=1&e=ghePDT&CID=cee21d85-538b-421b-9813-5aa1ad6e361b#deliver-ai "Permalink for Deliver AI")

The **Deliver AI** phase is focused on developing, iterating, and deploying the AI solution to ensure it meets both technical and business requirements. In this phase, the backlog is prioritized based on the use case’s potential return on investment (ROI), and the AI model is built and trained. The model is then validated against predefined performance criteria to confirm it delivers the expected business outcomes. Stakeholders collaborate on deployment activities and finalize enablement plans, ensuring a smooth transition to production.

As a result, this phase ensures that the AI solution is fully developed, validated, and ready for deployment, setting the stage for successful value realization.

## [**Operate AI**](https://sap.sharepoint.com/sites/210068/SitePages/E2E-AI-Lifecycle-Operate-AI.aspx)

[](https://sap.sharepoint.com/sites/210068/SitePages/E2E-Enterprise-AI-Lifecycle.aspx?csf=1&web=1&e=ghePDT&CID=cee21d85-538b-421b-9813-5aa1ad6e361b#operate-ai "Permalink for Operate AI")

The **Operate AI** phase focuses on the monitoring and maintenance of the AI solution to ensure it continues to deliver value over time. It integrates DevOps for AI practices such as ongoing monitoring, continuous integration, and re-deployments. This phase includes tracking the model's performance in production, resolving any operational issues, and ensuring that the solution remains aligned with business objectives.

**Value realization tracking** is a key, where the actual business impact and ROI of the AI solution are measured, enabling continuous improvement and optimization. ​

As a result, this phase ensures that the AI solution continues to deliver business value, drives ongoing optimization, and supports long-term success.
